# Copy: the Hebrew voice

The page talks like a calm expert friend: short, concrete, plural, no hype.

## Rules

- **Address in plural** (אתכם, שלכם, בודקים, משאירים) - gender-neutral and warm. Avoid imperative singular (בדוק/בדקי).
- **Verbs in present plural** describe what *we* do: "אנחנו בונים את התיק", "בודקים. בונים. מגישים."
- **One idea per block.** h2 ≤ 6 words, no commas in headlines (a lone comma in a big headline looks bad - rephrase or break the line).
- **Hero h1**: 6-10 words across 2-3 lines; can hedge honestly ("ייתכן שהעסק שלכם זכאי…") - honesty is part of the brand.
- **Lead**: 1-2 sentences, ≤ 30 words, says who does what and the end state ("…ומלווים אתכם עד שהכסף בחשבון").
- **Kicker**: 1-3 words naming the section ("מה מקבלים", "איך זה עובד", "אחרי הבדיקה").
- **CTA**: "ל" + noun ("לבדיקת זכאות", "לבדיקת התאמה"), the same everywhere; secondary forms "להתחיל ב…". The hover/alt line is a short present-plural motivator ("מתחילים עכשיו", "שתי דקות ויודעים").
- **Micro line under the main CTA**: effort/time reassurance ("בדיקה בשתי דקות").
- **Perks**: bold title (3-5 words) + one plain sentence.
- **Steps**: 2-4 word title + ≤ 8 word sentence. Four steps, the last one about the outcome.
- **Promises**: question-answer pairs - "אין X? אין Y." / "המלצנו לא ל…? …חוזר." - plus one plain truth ("חברה פרטית, לא הקרן.").
- **Numbers**: figure + unit word, with "עד" when it's a cap; always a footnote that states the limits.
- **Closing**: the time-to-answer promise ("שתי דקות והתשובה אצלכם").
- **FAQ**: real objections in the customer's words, answers ≤ 3 sentences.
- **Legal/footer**: say what you are *not* (not the government, not the lender), that people in images are illustrations, that third-party logos only identify parties.
- Numbers and Latin in RTL: wrap mixed runs with `dir="ltr"` / `<bdi>` when needed; use `₪` after the number ("290 ₪"); keep `"פריים + 1%"` together with no-break spaces.
- Use Hebrew typographic marks: gershayim ״ (בע״מ, מע״מ), geresh ׳ (ינו׳), maqaf ־ (מ־290).
